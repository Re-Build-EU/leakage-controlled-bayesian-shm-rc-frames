# Processed experimental evidence

This directory contains only processed quantities required to reproduce the simulation-to-experiment demonstration.

## Files

- `experimental_evidence_and_posteriors.csv`: source-supported categorical evidence and posterior outputs for EF, RF, DJ-1, and DJ-2.
- `experimental_posterior_summary.csv`: compact values used for the experimental posterior figure.

The physical experiments were previously published. Original publisher PDFs, photographs, and raw experimental records are **not** redistributed here.

### Experimental sources

1. Wahab AG, Zhong T, Wei F, et al. *Seismic performance enhancement of RC framed structures through retrofitting and strengthening: an experimental and numerical study.* Asian Journal of Civil Engineering (2023). DOI: 10.1007/s42107-023-00794-z. Source of EF and RF.
2. Li R, Liu L, Li Y, et al. *Experimental Investigate on Seismic Performance of RC Hill-Side Stilted Buildings Affected by Vertical Stiffness Irregularity.* Journal of Earthquake Engineering 28 (2023): 2273-2291. DOI: 10.1080/13632469.2023.2287136. Source of DJ-1 and DJ-2.

For EF and RF, the published ultimate roof displacements provide global roof-drift lower bounds of approximately 4.25% and 2.38%, respectively. For DJ-1 and DJ-2, the reported maximum positive interstory drift angles at failure correspond to approximately 4.55% and 5.00%.

Unavailable evidence is left blank/unobserved rather than imputed. In particular, source-supported section-curvature evidence and a member-response-regime observation were not assigned to the experimental specimens. Residual-capacity posterior values are retained in the data table for completeness but are not used as the principal experimental validation claim.
